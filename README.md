# Campus Delights

now need a movable and even animated 
Orbs: four soft blurred circles in green, blue, purple and orange drift slowly behind the content.

Cursor: the orbs shift slightly as you move the mouse.

Dots: a faint dot grid sits under the orbs for a tech feel.

Visibility: the orbs are fairly faint in the light theme and a bit stronger in the dark one, so the text stays easy to read.

Motion settings: if a visitor's device is set to reduce motion, the animation and cursor movement turn off.

Phones: the orbs get bigger and less blurred on 
for my current universitty wellfare project 
New section: "Where the real learning starts: the canteen"

It sits right under the hero and is an animated cartoon scene:

The canteen counter: a striped awning in your four accent colors, a "CANTEEN" sign and a steaming chai cup.

The samosa theft: the orange student sneakily reaches across the bench and grabs a samosa. It vanishes and then reappears a moment later.

The blame game: speech bubbles pop up in turn: "Bunk the 2pm lecture?", then "Who took my samosa?!", then "Wasn't me 😇".

The paper plane: the purple student throws a paper plane across the scene.

Reactions: the other students laugh and look around.

Background through the whole page

Paper planes glide across the screen.

Snacks and student items (🍟 ☕ ✏️ 🥟 📚 🎒) float slowly upward, alongside the drifting orbs from before.

All of this stops for visitors whose devices are set to reduce motion.

Ways to push it further

More mischief: a food fight, a student sleeping on a notebook, someone copying a neighbor's answers, a teacher peeking around the corner.

Make the canteen scene the full hero instead of a section below it.
